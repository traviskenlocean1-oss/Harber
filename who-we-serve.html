Who We Help

Whether you’re coming on your own, as a couple or as a family, we’ll meet you where you are.

Everything we do begins with the people who walk through our door, whose stories and courage give us the energy to keep making care easier to reach. 

For Individuals & CouplesFor Families & Teens

Things people often tell us in a first session

  • "I can’t turn my brain off at night. Every small thing turns into a worst-case scenario."

  • "I used to love my job. Now I feel tired all the time, even after a weekend off."

  • "We keep having the same argument, and neither of us knows how to stop it."

  • "Since the loss, everyone expects me to be fine again. Most days I’m just going through the motions."

  • "My teenager barely talks to me anymore, and I can’t tell if it’s a phase or something more."

  • "I’ve been holding it together for everyone else for so long. I’m not sure who looks after me."
