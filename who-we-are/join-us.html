Careers

Do the work you trained for, on a team that protects your time, your growth and your own wellbeing.

What is working at Harbor Mind like?

  • OUR CULTURE - 01

    A team that consults, not competes

    We meet every week to talk through cases, share what’s working and support each other on the hard days. No one carries a tough caseload alone.

  • OUR CULTURE - 02

    Clients first, always

    Every decision we make, from scheduling to session length, starts with what will help the person in the room.

  • OUR CULTURE - 03

    A deep personal connection to the work

    Many of us came to this field because therapy changed our own lives or the life of someone we love. That shared why keeps us steady.

  • OUR CULTURE - 04

    Room to grow your practice

    Paid training in EMDR, Gottman Method and DBT, supervision toward licensure, and a path to lead a program of your own.

We all have a why

We do this work because we know how heavy it can feel to carry things alone. Every session starts with the same promise: you’ll be heard, you’ll be safe, and you won’t be rushed.

Our benefits

We look after our clinicians the way we ask clients to look after themselves. Most of these benefits start on your first day.

Balance & Time Off

  • Caseloads capped so you have time to prepare, rest and write notes
  • Hybrid schedules with in-office and telehealth days
  • Paid time off plus a week off between Christmas and New Year’s
  • Paid days for your own therapy and wellbeing

Family Support

    • Paid parental leave for every parent, including adoptive parents
    • Flexible return-to-work schedules
    • Help finding childcare and elder care

Health & Medical

    • Health insurance premiums covered for full-time clinicians
    • Dental and vision coverage
    • Health savings account with a yearly practice contribution

Training & Growth

    • Paid certification training in EMDR, Gottman Method and DBT
    • Free clinical supervision toward full licensure
    • Yearly continuing education budget
    • Weekly team case consultation

Pay & Retirement

    • Competitive salaried and per-session pay
    • 401(k) with a practice match
    • Licensure and professional membership fees covered
