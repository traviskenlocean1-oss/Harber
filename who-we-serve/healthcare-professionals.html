Families & Teens

When one person is struggling, the whole family feels it. We help families and teens find their way back to each other.

Support for the whole family

Teen sessions are available for ages 13 to 19, in person in Miami or online across Florida.

Families come to us for all kinds of reasons: a teen who has pulled away, constant tension at home, a divorce, a move, or a loss that everyone is grieving differently. Whatever brought you here, you don’t have to figure it out alone.

For teens, we offer a private, judgment-free space to talk, alongside practical skills for anxiety, low mood and stress. Parents are part of the plan too, with regular check-ins that keep you informed while protecting your teen’s trust.

Family sessions bring everyone into the room, so each person can be heard and the whole household can practice new ways of talking and solving problems together.

We don’t promise quick fixes. We promise to listen, use approaches that are proven to help, and stay with your family until home feels steadier.

Teen anxiety and depression

School pressure, social media, friendships and big changes can leave teens anxious, withdrawn or irritable. We give teens a private space to talk, teach practical coping skills, and keep parents in the loop in a way that protects trust.

Family conflict

Constant arguments, divorce, blended families or a hard loss can leave everyone on edge. Family sessions help each person feel heard, lower the temperature at home, and build new ways of talking that actually stick.

Why early support matters

Teens and families face real pressure, and many people who need care still don’t get it. We’re working to make help easier to reach in Miami.
