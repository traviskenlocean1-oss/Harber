What We Treat

Whatever you’re carrying, there’s a proven way to make it lighter. Here’s where we help most.

Good therapy is personal and proven

At Harbor Mind, every plan pairs warmth with methods that have decades of research behind them. We start by understanding what you’re facing, then choose the approach that fits: CBT for anxious thinking, EMDR for trauma, DBT skills for overwhelming emotions, or Gottman-informed work for couples.

Your therapist explains the plan in plain words, sets goals with you, and checks progress every few weeks, so you always know where you stand and what comes next.

Where we help most

We treat the full range of everyday mental health concerns. These are the two we see most often.

Anxiety and depression

Anxiety is one of the most common mental health conditions in the U.S. An estimated 19.1% of adults had an anxiety disorder in the past year.¹ It often travels with low mood, poor sleep and burnout. With CBT and practical skills, most people learn to quiet the worry loop and feel like themselves again.

Trauma and PTSD

About 6 out of every 100 people will have PTSD at some point in their lives.² Trauma can leave the body on high alert long after the danger has passed. With EMDR and trauma-focused CBT, we work at a pace that feels safe, so painful memories lose their grip.
