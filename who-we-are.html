About Us

We’re a team of licensed therapists in Miami who believe everyone deserves a calm place to land.

Our promise to one client became a way of caring for everyone

Harbor Mind began with a simple frustration: people who finally worked up the courage to ask for help were met with long waitlists, rushed sessions and care that never quite fit. We built the practice we wished existed, where you can start within days, see the same therapist every week, and move at a pace that feels right.

How we work together

We don’t have a values statement on a wall. We have a way of working that every therapist here shares, built around one idea: you should feel safe, heard and never rushed.

  • 01

    We listen first

    Every plan starts with your story, not a checklist. We take time to understand what you’re carrying before we suggest a way forward.

  • 02

    We’re in this together

    Therapy works best as a partnership. You set the pace, we bring the tools, and we adjust the plan with you along the way.

  • 03

    We do things right

    We use methods with decades of research behind them, protect your privacy, and stay honest about progress, even when it’s slow.

  • 04

    We keep growing

    Our clinicians train every year in new approaches and meet weekly to talk through cases, so you get the benefit of the whole team.
